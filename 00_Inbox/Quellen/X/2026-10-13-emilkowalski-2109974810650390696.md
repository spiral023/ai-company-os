## Post-Text

As you invite more agents to our work, and create software factories to be more productive than ever, you’ll soon outsource everything to an agent, even the thinking.

The work you will do will feel frictionless, it will feel amazing on the surface, but once you go a bit deeper, it’ll feel extremely shallow. It won’t reflect the struggles you went through, your decisions, and ideas. 

It will no longer remind you of you and the work you aspire to. 

You will turn into just another extremely efficient producer of things, but not a creator like you were before.

Invite friction to your work.
