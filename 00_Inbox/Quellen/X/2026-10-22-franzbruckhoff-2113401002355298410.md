In a flawless codebase this directive will probably degrade the signal to noise ratio and give you false positives. 

It basically overrides the model's tendency for premature termination and forces the model into a multi-hop trace before reporting. So it overcomes an inherent laziness.

There are many other ways to go about this, but I found this to be the most compact one-shot approach. So far the results were pretty good.
